Nu, Pogodi!The Grand Chase

Be the clever Hare. Outsmart the scheming Wolf across fourteen levels of balconies, fairgrounds, highways, metro tunnels, circus rafters, a stormy cruise, a ski slope — and make it to the New Year's concert on time.

Free · runs in your browser · keyboard or touch · about 30–45 minutes

The story

It's the last morning of the year. The Hare is watering flowers on his balcony when the Wolf spots him from the street and fires a grappling hook. What follows is the longest, loudest chase of their lives: through an amusement park, down a highway, across a skyscraper in the making, onto a cruise ship, and up to a snowy mountain resort where the whole town is waiting for a New Year's Eve concert.

Nu, zayats… pogodi!

The route

Prologue

Balcony Escape

A long hop across balconies: crumbling ledges and the Wolf's swinging hooks.

Chapter 1

Trouble at the Fairground

Hall of mirrors, shooting gallery, Ferris wheel and carousel: find four golden tickets.

Boss

The Magician's Stage

Pull the levers and trap the Wolf in the magic box. Four times.

Chapter 2

Highway Mayhem

Bicycle vs. motorcycle, sports car and a runaway steamroller.

Chapter 2a

The Metro Tunnel

Sprint through a dark tunnel, then a hijacked subway train.

Chapter 2b

Construction Site

A very tall climb: cranes, girders and wrecking balls.

Chapter 2c

Under the Big Top

Tightropes, trapezes and crumbling circus planks.

Boss

Showdown at the Big Top

The Wolf is the ringmaster, faster and angrier. Five tricks to win.

Interlude

The Hospital Nightmare

Play as the Wolf, fleeing a giant Robot Hare through a surreal museum.

Chapter 3a

The Boardwalk

Bounce on umbrellas and hop across rafts above the sea.

Chapter 3b

The Sea Cruise

The Wolf punched a hole in the hull. Plug leaks, pump water, survive.

Chapter 3c

The Snowy Village

Slippery ice, thin ice and rolling snowballs.

Final

New Year's Showdown

Downhill ski chase against Snegurochka (a.k.a. the Wolf). Avalanche!

Finale

Happy New Year!

Fireworks, a giant tree, and one very tangled Wolf.
